## Flow clips and music

The opening shots of Sam, Mia and Grace were made in Google Flow on Eseosa's Pro plan.
Save Sam's clip as `clip-sam.mp4` in the capture folder. If a clip can't be downloaded,
save two stills as `kb-<name>-1.png` and `kb-<name>-2.png`, and compose.py turns them into
a slow push-in. The soundtrack is generated from scratch by `music.py`, so no licence is needed.
