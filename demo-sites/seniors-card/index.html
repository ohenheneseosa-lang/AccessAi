Seniors Card: eligibility, entitlements and application process

The Seniors Card program provides eligible residents with access to a range of concessions, discounts and benefits offered by participating government agencies and private sector partners, subject to the terms and conditions applicable at the time of use and in accordance with the relevant program guidelines as amended from time to time.

Eligibility criteria

To be eligible, an applicant must have attained the age of 60 years, be a permanent resident of the state, and not be engaged in paid employment for an aggregate of 35 hours or more per week, as averaged over the preceding twelve-month period, unless otherwise determined by the program administrator.

Entitlements

Cardholders may be entitled to reduced fares on public transport services, rebates on certain household energy charges, and discounts at participating businesses. Entitlements are not transferable and may be varied or withdrawn without notice.

How to apply

Applications must be lodged via the online application facility. Applicants are required to provide a valid Medicare card number and documentary evidence of residential address. Processing typically takes up to 15 business days, following which the card will be dispatched by post.

The card is issued free of charge. Replacement cards may incur an administrative fee.
